# Business board review

Use this workflow when a user wants a serious business review or decision, rather than a single blended opinion. It is a simulated advisory exercise, not a meeting of actual directors or a substitute for qualified advice.

## Establish the decision

Build a neutral brief from available evidence: the customer, problem, offer, delivery model, constraints, commitments, current stage and unresolved questions. Give changeable evidence a date. Separate verified observations, reported claims, inference and assumptions. Do not invent customers, revenue, costs, legal clearance or completed capabilities. Ask only when a missing decision materially changes the review; otherwise proceed and identify the uncertainty.

## Independent review

When independent subagents are available and authorized, assign non-overlapping roles appropriate to the decision. Cover strategy and customer value, financial and commercial viability, and execution and risk. Ensure the review considers legal and security questions where relevant without presenting it as professional advice. Give each role the same neutral brief and request its own findings, strongest objection, practical alternative, evidence gaps, conditions and recommendation before sharing other views. If independent agents are unavailable, disclose that limitation.

After the initial views, compare the strongest feasible version of the original proposal with a credible alternative. Where useful, ask reviewers to challenge one another and revise their positions. Preserve substantive dissent and uncertainty; evidence and consequences matter more than a vote count. Do not impersonate named people or imply their endorsement.

## Decision and follow-through

Lead with a recommendation and explain the few facts and tradeoffs that determine it. Provide actions ordered by consequence and dependency, an inexpensive test capable of changing the decision, verification criteria, unresolved decisions and a minority view when relevant. Separate work that can proceed now from gates for spending, live customer exposure, launch and deployment. Use existing ownership and planning systems. Do not invent dates, make external changes, create duplicate trackers or contact others without appropriate authorization.
